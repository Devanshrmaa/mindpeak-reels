

## Problem

The 3 auto-generated monthly blog posts (`autoBlogs.ts`) are extremely thin compared to the static posts in `blogData.ts`. Each auto post has only ~15-20 lines of vague, generic content with:
- No specific chapter names or weightage data
- No tables or structured data
- No internal links to practice pages, formula sheets, PYQ hubs
- No FAQ section
- No real exam-specific advice that varies by month
- Generic "top 5 tips" that repeat the same content regardless of month

Meanwhile, static posts like `top-10-jee-preparation-tips` have 100+ lines with specific chapter breakdowns, numbered strategies, and actionable content.

## Plan

### 1. Rewrite `jeeMonthlyPost()` with rich, month-aware content (~800-1000 words)

Each month gets specific content based on the exam calendar position:
- **Month-specific chapter focus**: Map each month to specific high-weightage chapters (e.g., March = Optics + Electrochemistry + Calculus revision)
- **Detailed daily schedule table** in markdown format
- **Chapter weightage table** with marks breakdown
- **Week-by-week plan** for the month (4 weeks of specific tasks)
- **Internal links** to `/jee-practice`, `/jee-pyq`, `/jee-rank-predictor`, formula sheets
- **Common mistakes section** specific to the month's focus chapters
- **FAQ section** (3-4 questions) at the bottom
- Read time updated to `12 min read`

### 2. Rewrite `neetMonthlyPost()` with rich, month-aware content (~800-1000 words)

- **Specific NCERT chapter references** per month (e.g., March = Human Reproduction + Ecology + Chemical Kinetics)
- **Botany vs Zoology split table** with chapter names and expected questions
- **NCERT page targets** with specific chapter names
- **Diagram practice checklist** with named diagrams
- **Internal links** to `/neet-practice`, `/neet-pyq`, `/neet-rank-predictor`
- **Previous year analysis** showing how many questions came from that month's focus chapters
- **FAQ section** at the bottom
- Read time updated to `12 min read`

### 3. Rewrite `monthlyStudyTipPost()` with rich content (~600-800 words)

Each of the 12 topic rotations gets a detailed template:
- **Research-backed tips** (not generic "use Pomodoro")
- **Specific examples** tied to JEE/NEET context
- **Do's and Don'ts table**
- **Student case study / example scenario**
- **Internal links** to relevant courses, study plan page, mentors page
- **Actionable checklist** the student can follow
- Read time updated to `8 min read`

### 4. Add month-specific data maps

Create data constants within `autoBlogs.ts`:
- `JEE_MONTHLY_FOCUS`: Maps month index (0-11) to specific chapter names for Physics, Chemistry, Maths
- `NEET_MONTHLY_FOCUS`: Maps month index to specific Biology, Chemistry, Physics chapters
- `MONTHLY_EXAM_CONTEXT`: Maps month to exam proximity context (e.g., "3 months to JEE Main Session 1")

This data drives the content templates so each month's blog is genuinely different and specific.

### Files to edit
- `src/lib/autoBlogs.ts` -- complete rewrite of all 3 post generator functions + add data maps

- The `BlogPost` interface
- The `getAutoGeneratedBlogPosts()` export signature
- The blog listing and blog post rendering views
- Integration with `blogResolver.ts`
