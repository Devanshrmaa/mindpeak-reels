## MindPeak vs Competition Positioning

### Where We Win

1. **1-on-1 intensity that Vedantu doesn't match** — Vedantu charges by the hour; MindPeak offers daily live sessions 6 days a week with the same dedicated mentor. This is structurally different and more deeply personalised.

2. **30-minute doubt resolution** — No competitor makes this specific, measurable, time-bound promise. This is a unique, defensible differentiator worth putting in the hero section, title tags, and every CTA context.

3. **Parent accountability layer** — Weekly analytics reports for parents are unique in the space. Vedantu, Origin Educare, and IITian Guide do not mention this. Parents paying ₹50K–₹1.5L per year want visibility. This is a conversion accelerant.

4. **AI-adaptive curriculum** — No Tier 2 competitor mentions this. BYJU'S implies it with "adaptive learning" but at batch scale. MindPeak does this at the individual student level — a significant technical differentiation to call out.

5. **Kota depth + online convenience hybrid** — ALLEN and Resonance own "Kota" as a brand signal. MindPeak explicitly claims "Kota-style depth with online convenience" without the relocation. This is a powerful message for Tier 2 and Tier 3 city parents.

## Schema Markup Action Plan

None of the competitors had schema explicitly visible in page source extracts — but all major players are likely using it. MindPeak should implement immediately:

| Schema Type | Pages to Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Organization` | Homepage | Establishes entity in Google's Knowledge Graph |
