## Vedantu One-on-One: Detailed Competitive Teardown

Vedantu is MindPeak's primary competitive threat in the one-on-one space. Key intelligence:

**Their framing (direct quote pattern):**
> "Not the rest of the class — you get a dedicated expert tutor who customises lesson plans, pace, and practice based on your strengths and weaknesses."

**Their 1-on-1 page structure:**
- Hero: "Book Free Demo" CTA + hourly pricing anchored (₹800/hr)
- Comparison table: Batch coaching vs 1-on-1 (5 rows)
- Feature table: Coverage, subjects, languages, session type, support
- "Vedantu Improvement Promise (VIP)" guarantee
- 600+ teachers, 4.8 average rating social proof
- 15-question FAQ section (FAQ schema likely implemented)
- CTAs: "Book Free Demo", "Find Me a Teacher", "One-to-One Learning (Classes start from ₹800 per hour)"

**MindPeak advantages over Vedantu's 1-on-1:**
1. **Daily sessions (6 days/wk) vs. per-hour billing** — Vedantu charges hourly with no commitment structure; MindPeak's daily cadence builds deeper mentor relationships
2. **Same mentor, every session** — Vedantu's "600+ teachers" network implies switching; MindPeak's dedicated mentor model is more accountable
3. **30-min doubt resolution guarantee** — Vedantu offers no time-bound guarantee; it is a measurable, marketable commitment
4. **Parent analytics** — Vedantu's "academic counselor + account manager" is opaque; MindPeak's weekly parent reports are concrete and differentiated
5. **AI-adaptive curriculum** — Vedantu's WAVE tech is for live session delivery; MindPeak's AI-adapted curriculum is a unique concept

**MindPeak action:** Create a dedicated `/mindpeak-vs-vedantu-one-on-one` page contrasting specifically on these 5 points. This captures high-intent comparison searches and conversions.

## Stealable Strategies — Specific Tactics to Adopt

### 1. Vedantu's Batch vs. 1-on-1 Comparison Table
Vedantu has a clean 5-row table on their 1-on-1 page contrasting batch coaching limitations vs. their personalized model. MindPeak's `/batch-vs-personal-coaching` page exists — add this exact comparison format with stronger copy (they say "limited doubt time"; say "0 doubt left unresolved in 30 minutes").

### 2. PracBee's "90% Rate 5/5 on Google" Specificity
Vague claims ("top-rated", "trusted") convert poorly. PracBee's specific percentage + platform anchor ("Google") is highly credible. If MindPeak's Google reviews support this, put the exact number and star rating on the homepage above the fold.

### 3. NEET Online Coaching's "40–60% Cheaper Than Offline" Calculator
This is a simple value-framing technique. MindPeak could add a "Cost Calculator" or comparison box on the pricing page showing: "Traditional offline coaching in Kota: ₹1.5L + ₹60K accommodation vs. MindPeak: [price]" — making the saving concrete and tangible.

### 4. PW's FAQ Schema + Article Schema
PW's dropper strategy article has a full FAQ section but (per analysis) no FAQ schema implemented. Vedantu's 1-on-1 page likely does. Adding FAQ schema to MindPeak's P1 content will earn rich results and PAA box inclusions.

### 5. Allen's ASAT (Free Scholarship Admission Test) as Lead Magnet
Allen uses a free scholarship test to capture leads — students who "qualify" get a discount and feel invested. MindPeak could run a "Free JEE/NEET Diagnostic Test" that produces a personalised weak-area report — this is both a lead magnet AND a demonstration of the personalised coaching value proposition.

### 6. Vedantu's "VIP Guarantee" (Vedantu Improvement Promise)
A named guarantee is a conversion accelerant. MindPeak's 30-minute doubt resolution is stronger than anything Vedantu publicly commits to — give it a name. "MindPeak Mentorship Promise" or "The 30-Minute Guarantee" in writing on the homepage and pricing page.

### 7. PracBee's Named-Student Social Proof Format
PracBee lists: "[Student Name] — [College Name, e.g. JSS Medical College]". This is more credible than "500+ students placed." MindPeak's success stories page should use this format: photo + name + rank + college + one-line quote.

### 8. Origin Educare's 2,000+ Word "1-on-1 JEE Coaching" SEO Article
Origin Educare wrote a dedicated 2,000+ word article targeting "best coaching for 1 on 1 live for IIT-JEE in India 2026" and it ranks. MindPeak has no equivalent article. Create one targeting the direct keyword "one on one jee coaching online india" — this is MindPeak's core commercial keyword and should be owned.
