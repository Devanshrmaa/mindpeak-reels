## Critical New Threat: PracBee "Pracbot" Launch

**This is the most important competitive development this week.**

PracBee has added **"Pracbot"** — an AI-powered instant doubt-solving assistant embedded in their 1:1 platform. They are now claiming: **"India's only Result Focused 1:1 Live Course."**

This is a direct attack on two of MindPeak's core differentiators:
1. AI-adaptive curriculum (PracBee now also uses AI)
2. Fast doubt resolution (Pracbot promises "instant" responses)

**Current PracBee social proof (updated):**
- 50,000+ registered students
- 2,000+ placed in top IITs/NITs
- 80,000+ hours of 1:1 teaching delivered
- 90% students and parents rate 5/5 stars
- Video testimonials with named students + admission cards (IIT Delhi CS, etc.)
- Dropper-specific "Last Lap Booster Program"

**MindPeak's counter-positioning:**
- Pracbot is AI chatbot for doubts. MindPeak's "< 30-minute human mentor response" is a named, accountable human — not an automated bot
- PracBee's 50,000+ "registered" students vs MindPeak's 500 "mentored" students — MindPeak should own the quality-over-quantity narrative
- PracBee has 15-day mentorship check-ins; MindPeak's daily 1-on-1 is more intensive
- **Messaging recommendation:** Counter with "Not a chatbot. A mentor. Every day." — make the human-vs-AI distinction explicit on the homepage
