Their domain authority (~70+) combined with these targeted pages makes them nearly impossible to displace short-term on broad keywords. However, Vedantu is a marketplace — they don't offer the truly personal, single-mentor relationship MindPeak offers.

**Physics Wallah** dominates batch/course-based 2027 prep keywords through sheer content volume and brand recognition. They are not a 1-on-1 competitor but crowd out informational SERPs.

**Allen & Aakash** dominate "best online coaching" listicles by being featured in every third-party ranking article. They don't need to rank themselves — AcademyCheck, Notesly, and CollegeDekho rank for them as aggregator/directory pages.

**IITIANGUIDE** is MindPeak's most direct competitor in the 1-on-1 niche. They rank well for "jee physics tutor online", "one on one jee coaching", "personalized jee coaching" etc. Their content strategy (topic-wise pages for each subject) is worth emulating.

| "one on one jee/neet coaching" | Dedicated 1-on-1 service page (Vedantu does this perfectly) |
