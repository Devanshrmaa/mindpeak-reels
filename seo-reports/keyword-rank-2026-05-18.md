## Competitor Insights

### Who dominates and why

**Vedantu** is the #1 threat for MindPeak's core value proposition. They have built dedicated pages for every niche variant of "1-on-1 coaching" (JEE, NEET, subject-wise, dropper, class-wise). Their domain authority (~70+) combined with these targeted pages makes them nearly impossible to displace short-term on broad keywords. However, Vedantu is a marketplace — they don't offer the truly personal, single-mentor relationship MindPeak offers.

**Physics Wallah** dominates batch/course-based 2027 prep keywords through sheer content volume and brand recognition. They are not a 1-on-1 competitor but crowd out informational SERPs.

**Allen & Aakash** dominate "best online coaching" listicles by being featured in every third-party ranking article. They don't need to rank themselves — AcademyCheck, Notesly, and CollegeDekho rank for them as aggregator/directory pages.

**IITIANGUIDE** is MindPeak's most direct competitor in the 1-on-1 niche. They rank well for "jee physics tutor online", "one on one jee coaching", "personalized jee coaching" etc. Their content strategy (topic-wise pages for each subject) is worth emulating.

### What content format Google wants

| Keyword Type | #1 Format |
|---|---|
| "best online coaching for JEE/NEET" | Course/enroll page from a big brand OR a listicle from an aggregator |
| "one on one jee/neet coaching" | Dedicated 1-on-1 service page (Vedantu does this perfectly) |
| "jee/neet [subject] tutor online" | Subject-specific tutoring authority page |
| "jee main 2027 preparation" | Batch enrollment page with 2027 branding |
| "how to prepare for X" | Long-form guide (2,000+ words), FAQ schema |
| "X vs Y comparison" | Comparison article with tables, clear verdict |
| Local keywords | Local landing page with city name in URL + content |

### Competitive gap MindPeak can exploit
None of the top competitors are **from Himachal Pradesh**. The Aspire Academy (aspireiit.in) is the only HP-based competitor with any SEO presence, but they are offline-focused. MindPeak can own the entire "HP online coaching for JEE/NEET" space if it creates local-intent pages for every major HP district.
