## Executive Summary

MindPeak's meta tag infrastructure has **one critical structural defect** that dwarfs all other issues:

> **`buildSEOLandingMetadata()` does not set `og:image`, `twitter:card`, `twitter:title`, or `twitter:description`.**

This means all 30+ pages that use this function (About, Mentors, Methodology, JEE/NEET subject coaching, dropper programs, crash courses, comparison info pages) will render as a **blank preview** when shared on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn. This is a conversion-rate disaster — parents sharing the school link to their network will see no image, no hook.

The second structural issue is that **comparison pages** (mindpeak-vs-allen etc.) also set no `og:image` in their page.tsx, even though the underlying comparisonData.ts has good titles and descriptions.

Beyond that, several **title tags exceed 60 characters** and get truncated in Google SERPs, and **two legal pages** have no brand identification.
