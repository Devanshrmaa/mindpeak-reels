## Executive Summary

MindPeak's SEO metadata is well-structured overall: most high-priority coaching pages have complete OG/Twitter tags, canonical URLs, and clear USP messaging. However, **8 tool/utility pages are completely missing OG and Twitter Card tags**, and **24 pages have titles exceeding the 60-character Google display limit** — these get truncated in SERPs, hiding important keywords and the brand name. Additionally, the `methodology` page is missing its brand suffix, and several page descriptions are too long (155+ chars).

The site's single biggest CTR opportunity: MindPeak's core USP — *personalised 1-on-1 coaching* — is already present in most titles and descriptions. The fixes below will sharpen that messaging and fix the structural issues causing SERP truncation.
