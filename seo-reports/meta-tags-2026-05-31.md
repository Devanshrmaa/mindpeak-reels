## Executive Summary

This audit builds on the May 17 baseline. The major structural fix (OG/Twitter tags in `buildSEOLandingMetadata`) was already implemented. This audit addresses two remaining systematic problems:

1. **34 title tags exceeding 60 characters** — causing SERP truncation, reducing CTR
2. **28 descriptions exceeding 155 characters** — causing ugly mid-word truncation due to `.slice(0, 155)` in `buildSEOLandingMetadata`, plus Google truncation in SERPs

And 9 individual page issues:
- Missing OG/Twitter tags on tool, practice, and comparison pages
- Missing brand name in 4 page titles
- Homepage OG title mismatch (page title vs layout default)
