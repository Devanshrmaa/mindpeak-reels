## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

### ❌ C1 — Homepage description too long (168 chars > 155 limit)
**File:** `app/page.tsx`  
**Current:** `"Crack JEE/NEET 2027 with personal 1-on-1 mentors from IIT/NIT. Adaptive study plan, 95% success rate, 500+ students. Book your free demo class today — zero commitment."`  
**Length:** 168 chars  
**Impact:** Google truncates with ellipsis; CTA "zero commitment" disappears.

### ❌ C2 — Homepage missing page-level OG/Twitter tags
**File:** `app/page.tsx`  
**Issue:** No `openGraph` or `twitter` block — page inherits the global layout.tsx defaults which use a different title and `hero-bg.jpg` (a dark background photo with no text). Social shares of the homepage show a mismatched title and a generic image.

### ❌ C3 — About page description truncated mid-word
**File:** `src/data/seoPageData.ts` → `aboutPage.description`  
**Current:** `"India's leading 1-on-1 JEE & NEET coaching. IIT/AIIMS alumni mentors, 500+ students, AIR 42 in JEE Advanced. Learn our story, faculty credentials & verified results."` (167 chars)  
**After `.slice(0,155)` in buildSEOLandingMetadata:** `"India's leading 1-on-1 JEE & NEET coaching. IIT/AIIMS alumni mentors, 500+ students, AIR 42 in JEE Advanced. Learn our story, faculty credential"` — cut mid-word ("credentials").  
**Impact:** Google sees a broken sentence; description fails basic quality checks.

### ❌ C4 — Courses page description grossly over-limit
**File:** `app/courses/page.tsx`  
**Current:** `"Explore 12+ personalized 1-on-1 JEE & NEET programs — foundation (Class 6–10), 1-year, 2-year, crash & Olympiad. 95% success rate, AIR 42 best JEE result. Book a free demo class."` (179 chars)  
**Impact:** 24 chars truncated; key CTA disappears.

### ❌ C5 — Study plan page missing OG and Twitter tags
**File:** `app/study-plan/page.tsx`  
**Issue:** No `openGraph` or `twitter` metadata block. When shared on WhatsApp/Instagram it shows a blank card or the global fallback.  
**Impact:** Tool pages are highly shareable; missing social card kills organic sharing.

### ❌ C6 — Global layout fallback title too long (77 chars > 60 limit)
**File:** `app/layout.tsx`  
**Current:** `MindPeak Institute — #1 Personalized JEE & NEET Coaching 2027 | 95% Success`  
**Length:** 77 chars  
**Impact:** This fires whenever a page doesn't set its own title (e.g. 404 pages, edge-case fallbacks). Gets truncated in SERPs.

### ❌ C7 — MindPeak vs Allen: two conflicting metadata sources
**File 1:** `app/mindpeak-vs-allen/page.tsx` → uses `comparisonData.metaTitle`: `"MindPeak vs Allen — 1-on-1 vs Batch Coaching | MindPeak"` (55 chars)  
**File 2:** `src/data/seoPageData.ts` → `mindpeak-vs-allen` entry, title: `"MindPeak vs Allen — Which is Better for JEE/NEET 2027? | Honest Comparison"` (76 chars ❌ too long)  
**Issue:** The page uses comparisonData (correct), but seoPageData.ts has a duplicate 'mindpeak-vs-allen' entry that would be served if the catch-all route ever resolves this slug. Conflicting metadata causes confusion; the seoPageData title is also 76 chars (too long).

### ❌ C8 — Free trial description 1 char over limit
**File:** `app/free-trial/page.tsx`  
**Current:** `"Experience personalized JEE/NEET coaching free. Meet your mentor, get a custom study plan, see our platform live. No card, no commitment — book in 30 seconds."` (158 chars)
