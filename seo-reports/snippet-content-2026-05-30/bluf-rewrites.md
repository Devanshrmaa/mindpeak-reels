## Implementation Pattern

Each rewritten paragraph:

1. **Answers the target query in the first sentence** with a numeric specific.
2. **Provides a structural answer** (steps, marks, hours, percentages) in the second or third sentence.
3. **Includes one "In our one-on-one classes…" E-E-A-T sentence** naming the relevant faculty member.
4. **Transitions to the page body** without forcing the reader to scroll through marketing copy.

Replace existing first paragraph in the corresponding view file. For `JEECoaching.tsx`, `NEETCoaching.tsx`, and similar views: the BLUF paragraph belongs immediately under the hero CTA, ideally as its own `<p>` with `class="bluf"` so the speakable schema selector can target it (see §9 of main report).

For SEO-data-driven pages (everything routed through `SEOLandingPage.tsx`), update the relevant `description` or first `SEOPageSection.body` field in `src/data/seoPageData.ts`.

Always update `dateModified` to `'2026-05-30'` (or the date of actual deployment) on every touched page — freshness signal compound is significant per §3.1 of the main report.
