<section id="kinematics-jee-strategy" data-updated="2026-05-30">
  <h2>How to solve Kinematics problems in JEE Main 2027?</h2>

  <p class="bluf">
    JEE Kinematics problems are solved by a 7-step structured method:
    define the frame, write equations of motion separately for each axis, mark known
    and unknown variables, choose between v=u+at, s=ut+½at², v²=u²+2as based on which
    variables are known, watch for relative motion, projectile-motion symmetry, and
    sign conventions. Most kinematics errors are not concept failures — they are
    sign-convention or variable-listing failures.
  </p>

  <ol>
    <li><strong>Step 1 · Define the reference frame</strong> — choose ground frame or moving frame. Mark positive direction explicitly with an arrow on your rough work.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 2 · Decompose motion into axes</strong> — for 2D problems, write x-axis and y-axis equations separately. Never mix.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 3 · List knowns and unknowns</strong> — write the 5 kinematics variables (u, v, a, s, t) for each axis. Circle the ones given; box the one being asked.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 4 · Pick the right kinematic equation</strong> — three equations, three pairings: (u, v, a, t) without s → v=u+at · (u, a, t, s) without v → s=ut+½at² · (u, v, a, s) without t → v²=u²+2as.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 5 · Watch sign conventions</strong> — most kinematics errors are sign errors. Acceleration due to gravity is +g downward or -g upward — pick one and hold it consistently for the entire problem.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 6 · Use projectile symmetry</strong> — time of flight T = 2u sin θ / g, range R = u² sin 2θ / g, max height H = u² sin² θ / 2g. Memorise these — they save 60-90 seconds per projectile question.</li>
    <li><strong>Step 7 · Handle relative motion explicitly</strong> — velocity of A with respect to B = v_A - v_B (vector subtraction). For river-boat and rain-man problems, draw the relative-velocity triangle before equations.</li>
  </ol>

  <p class="authority-note">

    method as a mandatory worksheet template for the first 30 kinematics problems — after
    that, the structure becomes automatic and Kinematics accuracy jumps from 60% to 95%+.
  </p>
